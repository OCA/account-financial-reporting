This module extends the functionality of the ``partner_statement``
module to automatically send a monthly Activity Statement to each
customer by email on the last day of every month.

The email includes a PDF attachment with the customer's Activity
Statement covering the full calendar month (from the 1st to the last day
of the month). The statement shows all invoices issued, payments
received, any outstanding balance carried forward, and an aging
breakdown.

Customers can be individually excluded from the automatic sending via a
flag on their partner record.

Usage
=====

The scheduled action *Send Monthly Account Statement to Customers* runs
daily and sends the Activity Statement to all eligible customers on the
last day of each month.

To exclude a customer from the sending, open the customer record, go to
the *Sales & Purchase* tab, and check *Exclude from Periodic Statement*.
