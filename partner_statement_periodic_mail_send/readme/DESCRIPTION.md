This module extends the functionality of the `partner_statement` module to
automatically send a monthly Activity Statement to each customer by email on
the last day of every month.

The email includes a PDF attachment with the customer's Activity Statement
covering the full calendar month (from the 1st to the last day of the month).
The statement shows all invoices issued, payments received, any outstanding
balance carried forward, and an aging breakdown.

Customers can be individually excluded from the automatic sending via a flag
on their partner record.
